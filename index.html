Campaign Problem Framing

The buyer problem we're solving

Our campaign leads with five interconnected pain points that resonate across all target personas. These are the problems we open every conversation with — not our product.

Misallocated capital

Investments are driven by urgency and politics, not value. The highest-impact opportunities are consistently underfunded.

No transparency

Decision rationale is locked in spreadsheets. Regulators, boards, and stakeholders cannot see how choices were made.

Strategic misalignment

Capital plans are built bottom-up. Strategic objectives are added afterward — not embedded in the decision logic.

ESG under pressure

ESG commitments cannot be traced to investment decisions. Boards face increasing scrutiny with no defensible data.

Fragmented systems

ERP, EAM, GIS, and risk systems hold siloed data. There is no unified view from which to make confident trade-offs.

The central campaign question we put in front of every buyer:
“Are we investing in the right things to deliver long-term outcomes?”

Persona Pain Chain

How the problem lands across our target personas

Our campaign targets three audience layers. Understanding how each persona experiences the problem helps us tailor messaging, content, and channel selection at every stage.

Executive
Planning
Operational
CEO / Board

Strategic alignment gap

Capital plans arrive without a clear line of sight to declared corporate objectives, ESG targets, or risk appetite.
Impact: Cannot assure stakeholders, regulators, or investors that capital is deployed strategically.
CFO

Unjustifiable spend

Investment proposals lack quantified value. Trade-offs between competing projects are opaque and politically driven.
Impact: No defensible basis for budget decisions under regulatory or board scrutiny.
CRO / CTO

Risk blind spots

Risk models live in isolation. There is no way to compare the risk consequence of funding versus deferring an investment.
Impact: Decisions are made without understanding the downstream risk exposure they create.
VP Asset Management

No single source of truth

Asset condition, performance, and risk data are spread across systems. Building a coherent capital plan requires manual reconciliation.
Impact: Plans are outdated before they are approved. Confidence is low at every level.
Capital Planning Manager

Constrained scenario modeling

Exploring funding scenarios means rebuilding spreadsheets from scratch. The process takes weeks and cannot respond to change.
Impact: Organizations cannot test the consequences of budget constraints or strategic pivots in time to act.
Regulatory Affairs

Defensibility under scrutiny

Regulatory submissions require evidence that investment decisions are data-driven and aligned to stated objectives.
Impact: Approvals are delayed. Organizations are exposed to challenge without audit-ready decision records.
Operations Director

Investment–execution gap

Capital plans are approved at portfolio level but do not translate into executable programmes at the operational level.
Impact: Work is planned against an approved budget that no longer reflects strategic priorities.
Maintenance Manager

Deferred work visibility

When capital is constrained, deferred maintenance accumulates without a quantified view of the risk being carried forward.
Impact: Decisions about what to defer are made on instinct. Risk backlogs grow invisibly.
Project Delivery

Misaligned priorities

Approved projects shift in priority during execution as strategies change, but the plan is not updated to reflect it.
Impact: Teams execute against an outdated plan. Strategic value is eroded during delivery.
